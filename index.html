Got Questions?

Frequently Asked HVAC Questions

Straightforward answers about cooling your home in Greater Phoenix.

How fast can a technician arrive at my home?

For emergencies (AC completely out during extreme heat), our average dispatch time across Phoenix, Scottsdale, and Mesa is between 45 to 90 minutes. For non-emergencies, we offer convenient same-day 2-hour appointment windows.

Why is my AC running constantly but not cooling the house?

In Arizona summer heat, this is usually caused by a dirty air filter choking airflow, low refrigerant from a microscopic coil leak, or a failing capacitor causing the compressor to overheat and shut off. Turn your thermostat to 'OFF' to prevent burning out the motor and call us immediately.

Do you charge extra for weekend or night emergency service?

No. We believe you shouldn't be penalized because your AC failed at 7 PM on a Saturday. We offer flat, transparent rates with zero hidden after-hours surcharges.

What SEER2 rating is best for Phoenix, Arizona?

In the Southwest region, federal regulations require a minimum of 14.3 SEER2 for split systems. However, with Phoenix electricity rates during peak summer hours, investing in a 16 to 18 SEER2 variable-speed unit typically pays for itself in energy savings within 3 to 5 years.
